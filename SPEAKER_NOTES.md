# Vibe Build Pitch — 90-Second English Script

Target length: **about 90 seconds** (roughly 200 spoken words). Keep the embedded videos playing silently; do not open the live demos.

## Slide 1 — Opening (0:00–0:14)

> Hi, I’m Jason. I like building interesting things and seeing how far an idea can go. I use AI to build fast, then learn deeply when I hit a real problem.

## Slide 2 — Skyline Rush (0:14–0:25)

> In one day, I built Skyline Rush, a procedural city with drones, gates, and bombs. AI turned my idea into something people could actually play.

## Slide 3 — The First Wall (0:25–0:37)

> But the city only looked real. The game could not tell roads from obstacles. Fixing that taught me procedural generation, flight dynamics, quaternions, and input abstraction.

## Slide 4 — Taiwan Drone Flight (0:37–0:50)

> Next, a fun question: can I fly over Taiwan? I reused the flight logic, rebuilt the world with Google 3D Tiles, and got it flying in two days.

## Slide 5 — Going Deeper (0:50–1:09)

> Then came the hard part. Average FPS looked fine, but slow frames made the controls feel bad. After improving the streaming system, p95-derived FPS went from 30 to 58, and p99 from 20 to 30. Predictable performance is better than peak performance.

## Slide 6 — Closing (1:09–1:30)

> I have over ten years in computer vision and product systems. Now I want to build something interesting in physical and spatial AI. If you have a real problem or an ambitious team, let’s talk. Maybe we can build something meaningful—and fun—together. Bring the problem. I’ll bring the build.

## Delivery Notes

- Speak clearly and directly. The language is intentionally simple and conversational.
- Aim for a calm pace of roughly 135–140 words per minute.
- Change slides immediately after each paragraph; do not wait for animations to finish.
- Pause briefly before the final two sentences, then leave the QR code on screen.
- If time is running short, remove “and input abstraction” on Slide 3.
